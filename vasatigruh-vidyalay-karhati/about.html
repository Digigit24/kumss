Our Vision

To establish Vasatigruh Vidyalay Karhati as a premier educational institution that empowers students through quality education, skill development, and ethical values.

We envision a learning environment that fosters innovation, critical thinking, and holistic growth, preparing students to become responsible citizens.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education in a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment. We are committed to building strong academic foundations and encouraging leadership.

By maintaining modern infrastructure and through continuous improvement and student-focused teaching, we prepare learners for academic success.

Our Legacy

History & Heritage

Founded in 1952 under the banner of Krushi Udyog Mul Shikshan Sansthan, VVK has consistently evolved while staying true to its founding vision.

Over the years, the institution has grown in infrastructure, academic offerings, and student strength. Today, it stands as a trusted educational pillar in the Karhati region.

Academic Excellence

Focus on Agriculture

One of the defining strengths of Vasatigruh Vidyalay Karhati is its emphasis on agricultural education.

Students are introduced to the fundamentals of farming, sustainable practices, and the role of agriculture in rural life. This hands-on exposure helps students understand the importance of agriculture while building respect for the land.
