About Our School

Vastigruh Vidyalay Karhati (VVK)

Vastigruh Vidyalay Karhati (VVK) is a Co-educational institution located at Karhati, Baramati in Pune district of Maharashtra, India. We are affiliated with Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE). Vastigruh Vidyalay Karhati offers secondary and higher secondary education.

Our Unique Approach

Agricultural Education & Holistic Growth

The highlight of our school is its agricultural education program. We teach students about farming, its importance, and its impact on the day-to-day life of villagers as well as the surrounding environment.

Research shows that learning experiences that usually take place outside the classroom have a long-lasting and positive impact on children. These experiences have the potential to develop a range of skills such as teamwork, collaboration, communication, and leadership.

"The children at our school learn to give a helping hand to their parents who mainly work in the fields, creating a strong connection between education and practical life skills."

01

Practical Life Skills

Connecting classroom knowledge with real-world farming applications.

02
