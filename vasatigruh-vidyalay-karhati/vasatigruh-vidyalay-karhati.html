Welcome to VVK

Nurturing Young Minds for a Brighter Tomorrow

Located in the heart of Karhati, Vasatigruh Vidyalay has been a beacon of knowledge since 1951. As a co-educational institution affiliated with MSBSHSE, we pride ourselves on creating an environment where curiosity meets character and academic excellence meets moral values.
