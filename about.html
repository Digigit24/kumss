About Krishi Udyog Mool Shikshan Sanstha

“Education opens up the mind, expands it, and allows you to improve your life in so many ways.”

KUMSS is an educational society that focuses upon the overall development of students. Established in 1951, great visionaries planted a seed of an idea. Now, that small plant has become a mighty tree under the leadership of KUMSS Ex. President and Ex. Dy.CM of Maharashtra Late Shri. Ajitdada Pawar since 2004.

Our Campus

Education for different ages, built on a strong rural foundation.

KUMSS is a growing education group where students of all ages learn and prepare for life. It includes Primary, Secondary and Higher Secondary Schools, English Medium and CBSE education, Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidyalaya, and a Private Industrial Training Institute (ITI). From strong foundation learning to modern classroom education and career-focused skill training, each institute plays a role in shaping confident, capable students. The focus is simple: strong basics, practical learning, and real opportunities, especially for rural students.

Ex. President’s Message

Words that reflect the purpose behind KUMSS.

“We are happy to gift a ‘Krishi Udyog Mool Shikshan Sanstha’ to the residents of Baramati (To the all baramatikars). There was hardly any campus repute in the western area of Baramati. People have to shift their base to main cities for quality education. I am delighted to have KUMSS which will be a great learning destination in the district and nearby areas and the students will not have to migrate to other places in search of quality education.

We strongly believe that every student is a unique person with his/her own set of abilities, needs and aspirations. We at KUMSS are committed to bringing out that uniqueness.

Besides academic excellence, KUMSS is committed to inculcate in all its students strong ethical values of integrity, politeness, kindness and respect for elders. I firmly believe that education should foster confidence, discipline, clarity in thoughts and decision making ability to set and achieve goals and above all, social responsibility as a lifelong process.

The unique KUMSS pedagogy backed up with meticulously laid state-of-the-art infrastructure brings excellence in learning, training and placement. KUMSS students are groomed to think out of the box and embrace articulation, innovation, teamwork, and leadership.

The challenge lies in utilizing the changing learning patterns with changes in the methods of instruction coupled with technological advancements, to create an educational experience that will influence the all-round development of students and uphold our commitment towards nation-building.”

Ex. President : Late Shri. Ajit Ashatai Anantrao Pawar
Commitment : Nation building
