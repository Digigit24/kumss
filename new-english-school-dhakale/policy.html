Student Code of Conduct & Institutional Policies — New English Medium School Dhakale

Discipline & Safety

Student Rules & Code of Conduct

To maintain a safe, disciplined, and productive learning environment, all students and parents are expected to follow the guidelines below.

1

Attendance Requirement: Every student is required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in the first semester and 75% attendance in the second semester.

2

Uniform & Identity: Students must come to school in the prescribed uniform every day. Each student is required to carry their I-Card at all times on campus.

3

Supplementary Education: All supplementary education coursework must be completed within the two semesters of the academic year.

4

Child Safety (POCSO): Any instance of molestation or harassment of boys or girls will be dealt with strictly under the POCSO Act (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences).

5

Parent Meeting Attendance: It is mandatory for parents/guardians to attend every scheduled parent-teacher meeting conducted by the school.
