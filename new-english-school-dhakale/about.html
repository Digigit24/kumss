New English School Dhakale

Welcome to Dhakale

Where Learning Meets
Innovation & Tradition

Established with a vision to empower rural students, New English School Dhakale has been a beacon of knowledge. We believe that every child is unique and deserves an environment that nurtures their intellectual, physical, and emotional growth.

Our curriculum blends academic rigor with creative arts and sports, ensuring holistic development under the guidance of expert mentors.
