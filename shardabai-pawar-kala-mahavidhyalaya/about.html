About Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidhyalaya

A focused institution for fine arts education, built to develop skilled, disciplined, and confident art educators. Here, creativity is trained with technique, practice, and real guidance.

Who we are

Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidyalaya is located in Karhati and operates under the Directorate of Art, Maharashtra State, Mumbai. That means your academic standards and certification remain recognized and reliable.

Since 1993–1994, the college has built a learning culture where imagination is respected, but craft comes first. Students learn how to see, how to draw, how to build form, and how to teach art with confidence.

What students gain

  • Studio discipline and regular practice habits
  • Stronger observation, form, and composition
  • Feedback-driven improvement and evaluation readiness
  • Confidence for classrooms, studios, and professional work

Art improves when your routine is steady. We train students to respect time, process, and consistency.

Academic Performance

Strong results. Real outcomes.

Over the years, the college has maintained consistent performance, with results reported between 90% and 100% from 1993–94 to 2023–24. The goal is not just passing exams. The goal is building educators who can guide the next generation.

What this performance indicates

  • Structured teaching and practical evaluation
  • Studio practice supported by faculty feedback
  • Focus on fundamentals before advanced work
  • Preparation that translates into professional confidence

Where students apply their skills

  • Schools and art education roles
  • Studio-based creative work
  • Community art and cultural programs
  • Professional practice with discipline and method
Courses Offered

Programs built for skill and teaching

Foundation Course (1 Year)

Designed for students who have passed 10th standard along with the Intermediate Drawing Examination. This course builds your base.

  • Drawing accuracy and control
  • Visual understanding and composition
  • Creative confidence through practice
  • Preparation for advanced art education

Art Teacher’s Diploma (A.T.D.) (2 Years)

For students who have completed 12th standard and the Intermediate Drawing Examination. This program prepares you for a professional career as a trained art teacher.

  • Practical art training with studio discipline
  • Teaching-oriented skill development
  • Assessment preparation and presentation
  • Professional readiness for education roles
