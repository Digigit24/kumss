Fine Arts - Karhati

About Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidhyalaya

Nurturing creativity with technique and tradition. We build skilled, disciplined, and visionary art educators through real-world guidance and focused practice.

Some Remarkable Activities

  • State Level Landscape Drawing Competition (Only for girl students) held by Nehru Centre, Worli (Mumbai)
  • Workshop of eminent artists from Pune & Mumbai under the leadership of Hon. Shri Tuka Jadhav (Head, S.N.D.T. Women's University)
  • Srujan Youth Festival State Level & Zonal competitions of Portrait, Landscape Drawing, Sculpture, Photography, etc. (2006-2007)
  • Srujan National Level Sculptors Camp (2016)
  • Taluka Level Drawing Competition for school students
  • Art Camp Kalaranga organized for school students
  • Exhibition-cum-sale of handmade greeting cards, Aakash Kandil & decorative lamps by students

Participation in Various Competitions / Achievements

Student Section

Our students have actively participated in State Level competitions organized by the State Government, Camlin Art Foundation, and various Art Colleges in Maharashtra. Many students have received awards. One student participated in a Group Exhibition at the National Art Gallery, New Delhi. Several exhibitions were also organized locally by our ex-students.

Staff Section

Our staff members have participated in National and State Level competitions and received awards, including State Art Exhibition (Artist Category), Bombay Art Society Exhibition, Camlin Art Foundation Exhibition, Chitari Art Foundation (Pune), and Indian Oil Corporation's National Art Competition.

Staff members were selected and participated in National Level Art Camps at Bilaspur (Chhattisgarh) and Vadodara (Gujarat). Various solo and group exhibitions have also been organized by the staff.
