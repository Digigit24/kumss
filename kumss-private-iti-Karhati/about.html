Our Journey

Empowering the rural youth of Baramati since the turn of the millennium.

KUMSS Private ITI Karhati was born from a singular vision: to bring industrial-grade technical training to the heart of rural Pune. Established in 2000 under the Krushi Udyog Mul Shikshan Sanstha (est. 1951), we have spent over two decades refining our methodology.

We don't just teach trades; we forge professionals. Our 2-year intensive programs in Electrician, Fitter, and Electronics are designed not just to pass exams, but to thrive in high-pressure industrial environments.
