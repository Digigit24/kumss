Our Journey

Empowering the rural youth of Baramati since the turn of the millennium.

KUMSS Private ITI Karhati was born from a singular vision: to bring industrial-grade technical training to the heart of rural Pune. Established in 2020 under the Krishi Udyog Mool Shikshan Sanstha (est. 1951), we have spent over two decades refining our methodology.

We don't just teach trades; we forge professionals. Our 2-year intensive programs in Electrician, Fitter, and Electronics are designed not just to pass exams, but to thrive in high-pressure industrial environments.

Core Philosophy

Our Vision

To be a world-class, student-friendly institution providing career-oriented skill development training. We aim to prepare masters and leaders in their respective fields.

Our Mission

To improve social well-being by providing industrial & skill development training for sustainable income generation. We tailor learning to individual student needs.

Our Values

Transparency, Integrity, and Concern for the Environment. We believe in instilling high moral values to produce responsible citizens for rural society.
