About KUMSS Private ITI Karhati

KUMSS Private ITI Karhati is committed to delivering quality technical education through modern infrastructure and well-equipped workshops that are regularly upgraded.

We transform 10th-class students into skilled professionals within a focused two-year training period, emphasizing both technical knowledge and personal growth.

Our training builds leadership, communication skills, creativity, discipline, and strong moral values. Individual counselling and continuous industry exposure ensure complete personality development.

Every student at KUMSS Private ITI is a valued member of our family, prepared to contribute positively to society and shape a successful future.
