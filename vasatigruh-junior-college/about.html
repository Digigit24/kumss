Our Story

Vasatigruh Junior College

Established in 2013, Vasatigruh Junior College operates under the Krishi Udyog Mool Shikshan Sanstha (KUMSS), a society with a rich legacy dating back to 1951.

Our college provides quality higher secondary education with comprehensive academic programs and holistic development in a nurturing, government-aided environment. We are committed to empowering students with knowledge, skills, and values needed for their future success.

KUMSS SINCE 1951

An educational society dedicated to the holistic development of students since 1951.
