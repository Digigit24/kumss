Admissions & Scholarships

Quality primary education at absolutely no cost — because every child deserves a strong start in life, regardless of financial background.

Opportunities for Excellence

Scholarship Programs

We actively guide and prepare students for a wide range of competitive scholarships and entrance examinations to help them reach their full potential.

NMMS

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship

A central government scholarship for meritorious students from economically weaker sections, providing financial support to continue education up to Class 12.

MTS

Maharashtra Talent Search

A state-level examination that identifies bright young minds and provides scholarships to encourage academic excellence across Maharashtra.

Scholarship Exam

Maharashtra State Scholarship Examination

The well-known 5th and 8th standard scholarship examinations conducted by the Maharashtra government to reward academic merit with financial support.

Navodaya

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test

Entrance examination for admission to Navodaya Vidyalayas — premier residential schools providing free quality education to talented rural students.

IAS Foundation

Indian Administrative Service Preparation

Early guidance and foundational preparation that inspires students to aim for civil services and leadership roles in public administration.

BDS & Beyond

Bachelor of Dental Surgery & Other Competitive Exams

Awareness and preparatory guidance for medical and professional entrance examinations, setting students on the path to healthcare careers.
