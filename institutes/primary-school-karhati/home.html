Welcome

More Than Just a School,
It's a Family

Since 1999, we have been the first step in the educational journey for hundreds of children in Karhati. We understand that primary years are crucial for shaping young minds.

Our approach is simple: Play, Learn, Grow. We move beyond textbooks to include storytelling, arts, nature walks, and hands-on activities, ensuring your child builds confidence and character.

Our Approach

Learning Made Fun

We combine traditional values with modern, activity-based learning to spark curiosity and creativity in every child.

Language Skills

Developing strong reading and speaking skills in English & Marathi through interactive storytelling sessions.

Playful Math

Understanding numbers and logic through toys, shapes, and puzzles — moving beyond rote memory.

Creative Arts

Weekly drawing, craft, music, and dance sessions to help children express their unique creativity.

Safe Travel

Secure bus facility covering Karhati and nearby villages with caring attendants for every trip.
