Scholarship Information

Our students have access to multiple government-recognised scholarship programmes that reward merit and support families.

Government Recognised

5th & 8th Std Scholarship

State Government scholarship examination conducted for students in 5th and 8th standard to identify and support meritorious students.

  • Open to all 5th & 8th Std students
  • Government of Maharashtra recognised
  • Merit-based financial assistance
  • School provides exam preparation support
National Level

NMMS Scholarship

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship provides financial assistance to economically weaker meritorious students at secondary level.

  • For 8th Std students from economically weaker sections
  • ₹12,000 per annum (Class 9 to 12)
  • Central Government funded (MHRD)
  • Mental Ability & Scholastic Aptitude Test
State Level

MTS Scholarship

Maharashtra Talent Search scholarship recognises talented students across the state through a competitive examination process.

  • Maharashtra State level examination
  • Boosts academic credentials
  • Financial rewards for top performers
  • Preparation guidance provided by school
National Level

NTS Scholarship

National Talent Search scholarship identifies and nurtures talented students through a rigorous two-stage national examination.

  • Prestigious national recognition
  • Two-stage selection process (State + National)
  • Monthly stipend for higher education
  • Supported up to PhD level
