About Our College

Our Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Objectives

The core values that drive every decision at Shardabai Pawar Kala Mahavidyalaya.

Our Vision

To create complete Art teachers by providing up-to-date knowledge and inculcating fine arts skills through direct and indirect experiences.

Our Mission

To nurture Art teachers with a profound aesthetic sense and creative excellence in the field of fine arts.

Our Objectives

To enable professional perfection, artistic happiness, and ideal teaching practices in every student we shape.
