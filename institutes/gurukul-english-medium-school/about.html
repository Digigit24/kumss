About

Gurukul English Medium School

Our Foundation

Our Vision

Our Vision is to develop well rounded Confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full Potential, to become responsible global citizens through child Centered Play based Curriculum that builds life Skills, independence, Confidence among them.

Our Mission

Gurukul English Medium School seeks to provide excellence in education. It seeks to make the learners confident, responsible, reflective, innovative and engaged citizens of India.

Why Choose KUMSS Gurukul?

Experienced Educators

Our faculty members are highly skilled professionals who bring their knowledge, passion, and commitment to every classroom.

Modern Infrastructure

Our campus is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, providing a conducive environment for learning, sports, and arts.

Holistic Development

From academics to sports, arts, and leadership training, we aim to develop well-rounded individuals ready for the world.

Focus on Values

We emphasize values like integrity, compassion, respect, and responsibility, creating students who are good human beings.

2021

Our History

Founded in 2021, KUMSS Gurukul English Medium School was born out of a vision to provide top-tier education that nurtures both intellectual and emotional growth.

From humble beginnings, we have rapidly grown into a trusted institution in Pune. We are known for our balanced approach, instilling life skills, and fostering a culture where every child is encouraged to dream big.

Academic Excellence

We offer a modern curriculum that challenges students to think critically. Through experiential learning and practical projects, we prepare the next generation of leaders.

STEM Education

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics aimed at fostering innovation.

Humanities

Understanding the world through History, Geography, and Social Sciences.

Languages

Mastery in communication through extensive Literature and Language programs.

Sports

Physical education programs designed to build teamwork, stamina, and discipline.

Creative Arts

Unleashing creativity through Visual Arts, Music, Dance, and Drama.
