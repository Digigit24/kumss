Academics

Building a Strong Foundation
for Future Leaders

At karhati Primary School, we combine traditional values with modern learning techniques to foster curiosity, confidence, and character in every child.

Our Academic Philosophy

We believe that primary education is the stepping stone for lifelong learning. Our curriculum is designed not just to teach subjects, but to teach children how to learn.

  • Focus on strong literacy and numeracy skills.
  • Activity-based learning to keep engagement high.
  • Personalized attention to ensure no child is left behind.
  • Integrating moral values into daily lessons.

What We Teach

A structured approach to holistic development.

Language Proficiency

We focus on Marathi & English fluency. Students engage in daily reading, phonics, and poetry recitation to build strong communication skills.

Mathematics & Logic

Learning numbers without fear. We use objects, beads, and games to make addition, subtraction, and tables easy to understand and apply.

Nature & Environment

Students explore the world around them through simple science experiments, garden visits, and learning about hygiene and seasons.

Art & Creativity

We nurture imagination through drawing, craft, clay modeling, and music, helping children express themselves freely.

Physical Fitness

Daily routines include Mass P.T., Yoga, and structured playground games (Kho-Kho, Running) to build stamina and discipline.

Sanskar (Values)

Character building is key. We teach respect for elders, honesty, and punctuality through morning prayers and moral stories.

How We Teach

Our teaching method is simple, consistent, and child-centric.

1

Concept Introduction

Teachers introduce new topics using real-life objects, charts, or stories to create interest.

2

Guided Practice

Students practice the concept in class with the teacher's help to build confidence.

3

Independent Application

Homework and activities allow students to apply what they learned on their own.
