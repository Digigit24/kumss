About the School

Established in 1999. We focus on a safe, caring routine and strong primary foundation.

We help children learn step by step with daily practice and simple activities. Along with studies, we build good habits like cleanliness, respect, and teamwork.

Strong Basics

Reading, writing, and numbers with daily practice.

Safe Campus

A supportive environment where children feel secure.

Good Habits

Respect, punctuality, and discipline through routine.

Learning Focus

Simple structure. Daily improvement. Better confidence.

Literacy

Reading practice, neat writing, and vocabulary building.

Numeracy

Number confidence with simple examples and practice.

Life Skills

Communication, teamwork, attention, and responsibility.
