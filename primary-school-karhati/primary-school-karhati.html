Primary School Karhati

Creating a safe, joyful, and creative environment where your child falls in love with learning every single day.

Welcome

More Than Just a School,
It's a Family

Since 1999, we have been the first step in the educational journey for hundreds of children in Karhati. We understand that primary years are crucial.

Our approach is simple: Play, Learn, Grow. We move beyond textbooks to include storytelling, arts, nature walks, and hands-on activities, ensuring your child builds confidence and character alongside ABCs and 123s.

Our Approach

Learning Made Fun

We combine traditional values with modern, activity-based learning to spark curiosity.

Language Skills

Developing strong reading and speaking skills in English & Marathi through interactive storytelling sessions.

Playful Math

Understanding numbers and logic through toys, shapes, and puzzles, moving beyond rote memory.

Creative Arts

Weekly drawing, craft, music, and dance sessions to help children express their unique creativity.

Safe Travel

Secure bus facility covering Karhati, and nearby villages with caring attendants for every trip.

Why Choose Us

Safe, Caring &
Disciplined

We know that sending your little one to school is a big step. Here is how we ensure they are in good hands, every single day.

Individual Attention

With small class sizes, our teachers act as second mothers, understanding the unique emotional and learning needs of every child.

Safe Campus

A fully gated campus with 24/7 CCTV monitoring and caring support staff ensures your child is always safe and secure.

Moral Values

We go beyond books. Daily assembly, value-education classes, and cultural activities help build a strong character foundation.
