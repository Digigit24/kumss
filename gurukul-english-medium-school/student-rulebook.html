Student Life

Guidance & Rules

A framework for discipline, excellence, and mutual respect to ensure a positive learning environment for every Gurukul student.

Code of Conduct

We expect our students to uphold the highest standards of integrity and discipline.

1. Attendance

  • Each student is required to attend school regularly.
  • More than 80% attendance is mandatory.
  • Permission must be obtained from the Head of Department, Class Teacher, or Subject Teacher for absence.

2. Discipline

  • Following school rules is mandatory.
  • Be polite and respectful to fellow students in your class and the school.
  • Respect teachers and other staff members.

3. Studies

  • Follow the curriculum and complete homework regularly.
  • Prepare properly for examinations.
  • Follow the instructions given by teachers.

4. Safety

  • Follow safety rules within the school and school premises.
  • Inform teachers or school administration immediately about your needs and problems.

5. Other

  • Do not damage school property.
  • Maintain cleanliness of the school premises.
  • Parents should cooperate with the school for the progress and discipline of students.
  • Parents must carry an identity card when visiting the school.

6. Admission

  • Parent's signature is mandatory on the admission form for new students.
  • The admission form must have the student's photo and necessary documents.
