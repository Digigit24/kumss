About KUMSS

“Education opens up the mind Expands it and allows you to Improve your life in so many ways”

KUMSS is an educational society which mainly focuses upon the overall development of students.

It established in the year of 1951, Great visionaries founder of the institute late Dr.Shri.A.J.Agarkar plant a seed of an idea. Now, small plant becomes a big tree with great leadership of KUMSS President and Dy.CM of Maharashtra Shri.Ajitdada Pawar from 2004.

70+

Years of Excellence

Our Core Philosophy

Building leaders through excellence in education and moral integrity.

Our Vision

  • Empowering Students: Developing masters and leaders through skill development.
  • World-Class Institution: Providing career-oriented training globally.
  • Global Leadership: Leading the way in education and innovation.
  • Well-rounded Individuals: Creating a safe, welcoming environment.

Our Mission

  • Empowering Learners: Tailoring quality services to individual needs.
  • Social Well-being: Training for sustainable income generation.
  • High-Quality Education: Safe, inclusive, life-long learning.
  • Practical Focus: Intellectually stimulating practical learning.
