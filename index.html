About KUMSS

"Education opens up the mind, expands it and allows you to improve your life in so many ways"

KUMSS is an educational society which mainly focuses upon the overall development of students.

It established in the year of 1951, Great visionaries founder of the institute late Dr. Agarkar plant a seed of an idea. Now, small plant becomes a big tree with great leadership of KUMSS Ex. President and Ex. Dy.CM of Maharashtra late Shri.Ajitdada Pawar from 2004.

Years of Excellence
Who We Are

Our Core Philosophy

Building leaders through excellence in education and moral integrity.

Our Vision

  • To empower students through quality education in skill development and technical education to prepare a master and leader in his field.
  • To empower and position the organization as a world class progressive, cost effective and student friendly institution providing career oriented skill development training.
  • To be a leading global organization that provides a transformative education to create leaders and innovators, and generates new knowledge for society and industry.
  • Our vision is to develop well rounded, confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality education, training and expert advice.
  • To increase the employability of students and ensuring that our workforce is of a world class standard.
  • To create a collaborative, inclusive and creative environment for research and education.
  • The primary purpose of the institute is to provide a learning environment in which faculty, staff and students can discover, examine critically, preserve and transmit the knowledge and values that will help to ensure the survival of this and future generations.

Our Values

Integrity

Acting with honesty and strong moral principles in everything we do.

Excellence

Striving for the highest standards in teaching, learning and service.

Respect

Valuing every individual and fostering a culture of dignity and inclusion.

Innovation

Encouraging creative thinking and embracing new ideas to solve challenges.

Compassion

Supporting one another with empathy, care, and genuine concern.

Ethics

Upholding the principles of fairness, accountability and responsible conduct.

We are committed to instilling these core values in every student.
