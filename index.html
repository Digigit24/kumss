About KUMSS

"Education opens up the mind, expands it and allows you to improve your life in so many ways"

KUMSS is an educational society which mainly focuses upon the overall development of students.

It established in the year of 1951, Great visionaries founder of the institute late Dr.Shri.A.J.Agarkar plant a seed of an idea. Now, small plant becomes a big tree with great leadership of KUMSS President and Dy.CM of Maharashtra Shri.Ajitdada Pawar from 2004.

70+

Years of Excellence

Our Core Philosophy

Building leaders through excellence in education and moral integrity.

Our Vision

  • To empower students through quality education in skill development and technical education to prepare a master and leader in his field.
  • To empower and position the organization as a world class progressive, cost effective and student friendly institution providing career oriented skill development training.
  • To be a leading global organization that provides a transformative education to create leaders and innovators, and generates new knowledge for society and industry.
  • Our vision is to develop well rounded, confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential. We will do this by providing a welcoming, happy, safe, and supportive learning environment in which everyone is equal and all achievements are celebrated.

Our Mission

  • To provide our learners with quality learning services that caters to their individual needs and requirements.
  • To improve the economic and social well-being of youth through industrial & skill development training for sustained income generating activities throughout their lives.
  • Our Mission is to provide high quality education, safe, respectful and inclusive environment that builds a foundation for life-long learning.
  • Focus on practical aspects of the course material so as to make learning a meaningful and interesting experience in our intellectually stimulating campus.
  • Work to inculcate high moral and ethical values amongst the students to make them responsible citizens and good human beings.
