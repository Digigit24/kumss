Welcome to Vasatigruh Primary

Building Strong Foundations for Young Minds

Located in the heart of Karhati, Vasatigruh Primary School has been nurturing young learners since 1952. As a government-aided co-educational primary institution, we focus on creating a warm, engaging atmosphere where children develop a love for learning alongside strong character and foundational skills.

Our Learning Approach

Nurturing Curiosity Through Activity-Based Learning

We believe that young children learn best through exploration and hands-on activities. Our curriculum blends traditional academics with creative, experiential learning methods that spark curiosity and build confidence from an early age.

"Our little learners don't just memorize — they discover, create, and grow. Every classroom activity is designed to build a strong foundation for lifelong learning."

Strong Academics

Comprehensive primary curriculum covering language, mathematics, science, and social studies.

Creative Expression

Art, music, and craft activities that encourage imagination and self-expression.

Physical Development

Sports, yoga, and outdoor play that promote fitness and healthy habits.

Value Education

Building character through moral stories, community service, and respectful conduct.
