About Our College

Establishment

Founded in 1993-94, serving as a beacon of artistic excellence for over 30 years.

Affiliation

Directly affiliated with the Directorate of Art, Maharashtra State, Mumbai.

Nature

A premier Non-government grant-in-aid art college focused on professional growth.

Academic Programs

Specialized courses designed to mold the next generation of Art Educators.

  • Duration: 1 Year
  • Intake: 15 Students

Our Guiding Principles

Our Vision

To create complete Art teachers by providing up-to-date information and inculcating fine arts skills through direct and indirect experiences.

Our Mission

To nurture Art teachers with a profound aesthetic sense and creative excellence in the field of fine arts.

Our Objectives

To enable professional perfection, artistic happiness, and ideal teaching practices in every student.
